===== Send, View, and Manage Intake Forms https://help.clio.com/hc/en-us/articles/9284567246747-Send-View-and-Manage-Intake-Forms updated 2026-09-28T23:27:43Z
Send intake forms
When preparing a form to send to a prospective client, you can add the form to a matter as an individual intake process item, as part of a checklist template grouping, or prepare a form from the Forms tab. Once sent, your prospective client can start the intake forms, save their progress, and return to the form without losing their answers. 
Note: An intake form link is valid for 14 days. Once expired, your client can click the link in the form email to receive a new email with an active link to the form. After 30 days, that link is permanently expired and you will need to resend the form instead.

Send Quick Forms
A Quick Form is a combination of an intake form and a new contact with a new matter. If you choose to prepare and send a Quick Form, Clio Grow will also create a new contact and matter for your prospective client. Once sent, your prospective client can start the intake forms, close it, and return to the form without losing their answers.
- Click the down arrow next to the Quick Intake and select Quick Form.
- 
Select the form template from the dropdown.
Note: If your form template contains more than one set of contact information, it will not show up in this list. This is to prevent potential confusion about which contact is your client compared to other contacts associated with the matter. If you want to collect information about additional contacts using the Quick Forms option, create fields for contacts using regular, rather than contact-type fields. Learn more here.
- Enter the lead's first and last names and the the matter type and status.
- Choose to Fill out form or Send to client.
- Click Fill out form or Send form.
- 
Fill out form: If you select this option, you can fill out and submit the form immediately. You can use this option if your prospective client is at your office or on the phone with you.
- 
Send form: If you select this option, you can send the form to your prospective client by email and/or text. Learn more about text notifications and reminders in Clio Grow here.
Set due dates for intake forms
After preparing and sending an intake form, you can set a due date for the form's completion.
- Go to the matter's Intake process section.
- Find the form.
- Click the Options dropdown and select Set Due Date.
- Select the due date and then click Update Due Date.
Send reminders for intake forms
After preparing and sending an intake form, you can send your prospective client an email and/or text reminder to complete the intake form.
- Go to Forms.
- Select the Pending subtab.
- 
Optional: Click Apply filters to narrow your results.
- Click the form's name to view the form.
- Click the Actions dropdown and select Send Reminder.
- Under Email, select the email template from the dropdown menu or add your custom message.
- Click Send Reminder.
Resend intake forms
If your client's intake form link has expired or if they are unable to find the email containing the form's link, you can resend the intake form to them by email or text message. 
Note: When you first send your client an intake form, the form link is valid for 14 days. If your client has access to the email containing the form link, they can click the link to receive an email containing a refreshed link. After 30 days, the form link is permanently expired and you will need to resend the form with a new link.
- Go to the matter's Intake process section.
- Find the form task.
- Click the Options dropdown and select Resend Form Link.
- Under Email, select the email template from the dropdown menu or add your custom message.
- Under Text message, the option to send the form via text message is enabled by default, but you can toggle this off if you only want to send the form by email. Learn more about text notifications and reminders in Clio Grow here.
- Click Send form.
View submitted or pending intake forms
You can view an intake form that has been submitted or is pending submission from the main Forms tab or from the matter's page.
View Forms tab Via matter's page
- Go to Forms.
- Select either the Pending or Submitted subtabs.
- 
Optional: Click Apply filters to narrow your results.
- Click the form's name to view the form.
- Go to the matter.
- Select the Files subtab.
- Under Forms, hover over the intake form and select Download to view the form as a downloaded PDF or select View Form Details to view the form in your web browser.
Edit submitted intake forms
Once a prospective client submits an intake form, they cannot edit the form. If the form is missing information, an administrator can edit the form to add additional information. Administrators can edit the form from the main Forms tab or from the matter's page. Once the form is saved, it will be saved as a second PDF within the matter. This means that you can view both the original form that the prospective client submitted and the form that the administrator edited.
Forms tab Matter's page
- Go to Forms and select the Submitted subtab.
- 
Optional: Click Apply filters to narrow your results.
- Click the form's name.
- Click the Actions dropdown and select Edit Form.
- Make any necessary edits and then click Submit.- Once the edits are submitted, the form will be saved as a second PDF, in addition to the original form that the prospective client submitted. You can also view a record of the submission as a new entry in the matter's Timeline.
- Go to the matter and select the Workflow subtab.
- Find the submitted intake form.
- Click the Options dropdown and select Edit Form.
- Make any necessary edits and then click Submit.- Once the edits are submitted, the form will be saved as a second PDF, in addition to the original form that the prospective client submitted. You can also view a record of the submission as a new entry in the matter's Timeline.
Download completed intake forms
You can download PDF copy of a completed intake form from the main Forms tab or from the matter's page.
Via Forms tab Via matter page
- Go to Forms.
- Select the Submitted subtab.
- 
Optional: Click Apply filters to narrow your results.
- Click the form's name to view the form
- Click the Actions dropdown and select Download PDF.
- Go to the matter.
- Select the Files subtab.
- Under Forms, hover over the intake form and select Download.
Delete pending or submitted intake forms
- Go to the matter's Intake process section.
- Find the form.
- Click the Options dropdown and select Delete Form.
- Click Remove Item to confirm.
