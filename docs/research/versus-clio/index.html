Why they'd choose us

  1. Every regulated step would wait in one queue for a named approver.
  2. Ontario court rules, By-Law 9 and Form 9A planned out of the box.
  3. Canadian bank payments (EFT, PAD, Interac) planned, not cards only.
  4. Storage and AI in Canada, logged per call (once contracted).
  5. One CAD price with AI included (price to set).

Why they wouldn't yet

  1. Moving years of records out of Clio is risky.
  2. No certifications, SLA, customers or support yet.
  3. Clio Work's Canadian research corpus and Clio Accounting.
  4. Missing day-to-day integrations and native mobile.

Other rivals watch

LEAPAI rival sold in Canada: Matter AI answers over a matter's documents with cited sources21; LawY lawyer-verified answers and AI prompts for Ontario and BC court forms22 (medium confidence). Ontario court-form help is not our edge.
SmokeballArchie agentic assistant creates tasks, memos and events with user approval23; not sold in Canada. Per-action approval exists elsewhere; our planned claim is one firm-wide queue with preparer/approver separation.

Entry plan: run alongside Clio first, starting with intake, deadlines and time recovery. Add billing and trust once the firm trusts us.
