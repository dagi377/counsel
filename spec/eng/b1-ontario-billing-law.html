Corrections to the earlier spec

  • s.33 does not point to Courts of Justice Act rates. Since 2009 (S.O. 2009, c. 33, Sched. 2, s.70), s.33(3) only requires the rate to be shown on the bill, and s.33(4) lets the assessment officer disallow or vary it. s.33(5) allows a regulation to set a maximum rate; check whether one has been made (verify). Use the CJA s.127 rates only as a contractual benchmark written into the retainer (BIL-L-010).
  • The $7,500 cash limit is in By-Law 9, not By-Law 7.1 (BIL-L-039). By-Law 7.1 covers client identification and verification (BIL-L-043).
  • Contingency assessment windows are 30 days after delivery or 1 year after payment (s.28.1(11)(a)); for court-approved agreements above the maximum, the window is 6 months (O. Reg. 563/20 s.5). The general bill windows are 1 and 12 months (ss.3-4).
  • The LSO Standard Form applies only to percentage contingency agreements. Non-percentage agreements must instead contain eight prescribed statements (O. Reg. 563/20 s.7).

The software must NOT

  • Pay fees from trust for a bill that has not been delivered, or for more than the delivered bill's balance. (BIL-L-035)
  • Let any client trust ledger go below zero, or use one client's trust money for another. (BIL-L-036)
  • Withdraw trust money in cash or to 'cash'/'bearer'. (BIL-L-037)
  • Execute a trust transfer without a licensee (or approved bonded) authorizer and a stored authorization. (BIL-L-038)
  • Accept cash bringing the client-file aggregate to 750000 cents or more without a recorded exception. (BIL-L-039)
  • Charge interest before delivered_at + 1 month, or at a rate not printed on the bill, or without client-agreed terms. (BIL-L-008..010)
  • Deliver an unsigned bill or let the agent sign; agent may draft, never sign or approve. (BIL-L-001)
  • Edit, renumber or delete a delivered bill, its dockets or the bill register; corrections are credit notes. (BIL-L-006, 040)
  • Mix fees and disbursements in one line or subtotal. (BIL-L-028)
  • Allow contingency fees in family, criminal or quasi-criminal matters, or a percentage CFA off the LSO standard form without a recorded exemption. (BIL-L-016, 017)
  • Bill a contingency fee greater than the client's recovery (incl. costs, excl. disbursements and taxes). (BIL-L-020)
  • Pay referral fees or fee shares to non-licensees, or referral fees above the cap. (BIL-L-031, 032)
  • Tell a client the assessment right has 'expired'; after 12 months it needs special circumstances, it does not vanish. (BIL-L-005)
  • Purge financial records before the By-Law 9 retention period. (BIL-L-041)
  • Charge HST on agent disbursements or on interest. (BIL-L-048, 049)
