Adversarial review: architecture

Scope: a1 uptime, a2 interfaces + openapi.yaml, a3 migration, a4 data model (loaded into a throwaway Postgres 15: 0001 applies cleanly, 180 tables, smoke file raises the 6 expected errors), o1 build guide and tasks, o2 spec lint, Paploo ADR 0014. 26 findings: 5 critical, 12 high, 7 medium, 2 low.

Confirmation of o1 CF-01..18

  • Confirmed in the live DDL: CF-01 (share_ppm, markup_ppm, interest_ppm, contingency_ppm), CF-02 (gl.journal_entry has no book column), CF-03 (no outbox, inbox, idempotency, effect_executions, gate_versions, payment_event, interest_charge, client_credit, write_off or feature_flag table), CF-08 (bill_line_time_entry_id_key and bill_line_expense_id_key are unconditional), CF-09 (billing.bill has no interest_rate_bp, place_of_supply, signed_by or signature_hash), CF-17 (RLS on exactly 10 tables).
  • CF-04's proposed fix cannot be applied as written. The gate_code CHECK rejects kind slugs and the renamed G-xx ids (RV2-001).
  • CF-17 understates the gap. 40 tables that have a matter_id column have no RLS, and FORCE is missing (RV2-014).
  • CF-03 needs more than tables. Once-only unique keys and an effect_execution record are also needed (RV2-004).

Highest-risk items

  • Gate engine: approvals are not tied to the payload version, expiry is not enforced and status can be changed directly (RV2-002, RV2-003, RV2-017, RV2-018).
  • Trust migration double-counts balances, and the history rows cannot be inserted (RV2-005). The first reconciliation after cut-over is blocked (RV2-006).
  • Uptime: the single sync standby is a write single point of failure. The documents RPO and the all-cells RTO are not backed by the design (RV2-008..010).
  • Paploo ADR 0014 and Counsel each have a gate engine, and neither is named as the authority (RV2-015).
