Billing engine: ledgers, calculation rules, state machines, invariants

Design for Counsel's billing core. Applies to legal-spec/billing.json, time.json and the billing side of trust.json. Every number below was computed by a reference implementation in integer cents, and every journal entry is checked to balance. Machine-readable copy: b3-billing-engine.json. Rule ids: BIL-C (calculation; BIL-C-2xx apply b2's BIL-T tax rules), BIL-I (invariant), BIL-J (journal), BIL-X (worked example).

Conventions

  • money: int64 CAD cents
  • rate: int cents per hour
  • percent: int ppm (1% = 10000), same unit as tax rates (CF-01)
  • tax_rate: int ppm
  • rounding: half away from zero, rhu(num, den); tax once per bill per tax line
  • local_date: delivery_local_date = delivered_at AT TIME ZONE America/Toronto; months_after clamps to month end
  • time_zone: America/Toronto
  • dates: ISO 8601
  • rhu(num, den): (2*num + den) div (2*den) for num >= 0; mirrored for negatives. Integer only.

Decisions that resolve conflicts in the spec

  • Rate precedence: the 8-tier chain in BIL-C-003. It merges the brief's order (matter > client > user group > user default) with time.json's order (manual > flat > matter > client/table > activity > default). Within a level, a user rate beats a role-group rate.
  • Rounding: half away from zero is used everywhere, not banker's rounding (BIL-C-002). Time is rounded once per entry when it is saved, never again on the bill. Tax is rounded once per tax line per bill.
  • Discount order: the spec says discounts apply 'after credit notes, before tax'. On a draft no credit notes exist (BIL-C-080), so the order is: line write-down, then line discount, then bill discount, then tax.
  • Allocation: within a bill, fees then disbursements then interest, so principal is paid before interest. Across bills, the oldest due bill is paid first. Both are configurable under G-B8 (BIL-C-060, BIL-C-062).
  • Credit-note excess and overpayments: excess goes back to trust first, then to client credit (BIL-C-081). Client credit is client money and must be applied, refunded or moved to trust within 5 business days (BIL-C-067).
  • Trust withdrawals post at execution and authorized amounts are held against the ledger and the bill, so the same money cannot be authorized twice (BIL-C-053, BIL-C-054).
  • Units: every rate and percentage is ppm (CF-01).
  • Place of supply changed after approval: the bill does not return to draft. Before delivery it is voided and rebuilt; after delivery it gets a full credit note and a new bill (BIL-C-207).
  • Tax law: rates, place of supply, invoice content and bad-debt rules belong to b2-sales-tax (BIL-T-*). Billing-law clocks belong to b1-ontario-billing-law (BIL-L-*). This document only applies them, in BIL-C-200 to BIL-C-207. Because of that: tax on each payment is proportional (b2 BIL-T-082), so the fees-first order only affects how pre-tax revenue is reported; the bad-debt tax adjustment equals the bill's open tax; interest carries no tax; card surcharges are taxed like the bill they pay (BIL-C-208, verify); interest starts at max(due date, months_after(Toronto delivery date, 1)) at a rate agreed in writing and snapshotted at approval (b1 BIL-L-008 to BIL-L-010).
  • Interest is never a line on an approved bill. It accrues as InterestCharge rows (BIL-C-072).
  • WIP is a memo pair inside the operating book. Revenue is recognised at bill approval (the tax point), at recorded value. Write-downs and discounts post to contra-revenue OP-4900, so realization can be read from the ledger.
