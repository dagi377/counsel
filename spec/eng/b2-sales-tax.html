Sales tax on invoices (GST/HST, PST, QST, RST) for an Ontario firm

Scope: tax a Counsel bill must charge, by where the service is supplied, plus disbursements, discounts, credit notes, rounding, tax point, invoice content and bad debts. Money in integer cents; rates in parts per million (13% = 130000); dates in America/Toronto. Machine copy: b2-sales-tax.json (36 rules, 49 decision-table rows). Rules marked (verify) rest on a source not fully read in this pass.

Evaluation order

  1. Non-resident? Apply Sch. VI Part V s.23 zero-rating unless an exclusion hits (BIL-T-015).
  2. Litigation already started before a provincial court or tribunal? Use that province (BIL-T-012).
  3. Service in relation to real property? Use the province where the property is (BIL-T-011).
  4. Else the client's Canadian address, most closely connected if several (BIL-T-013); none on file means Ontario (BIL-T-014).
  5. Add the provincial PST, RST or QST line only if that tax applies and the firm is registered for it (BIL-T-020).
  6. Per line: AGENT disbursements stay outside the tax base; OWN disbursements and fees are taxed (BIL-T-030).
  7. Discounts reduce the base, then tax is computed per tax code and rounded once (BIL-T-040/042).
