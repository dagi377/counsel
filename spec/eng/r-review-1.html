Adversarial review: money and law

Scope: b1, b2, b3 (and its reference engine), money tables in a4, money endpoints in a2, the o1 conflict register CF-01..18, and legal-spec billing/time/trust. Every worked example in b3 (BIL-X-001..022) was recomputed: all journals balance and the cents reconcile. The defects are in the law, in rules that disagree across files, and in the trust and data-model guards.

Counts: critical 2, high 11, medium 9, low 3. Items marked (verify) need the official text checked before code ships.

Most serious

  • RV1-001: the non-resident zero-rating exclusions are wrong. A foreign corporation's Ontario lawsuit would be billed with no HST.
  • RV1-002: between authorization and posting, trust money can be paid out twice. Nothing reserves amounts that are authorized but not yet posted.
  • RV1-003, RV1-004: nothing enforces uncleared funds, and the $7,500 cash limit is off by one cent.
  • RV1-006, RV1-007: client money is parked in operating as "client credit", and card surcharges are treated as tax-exempt.
